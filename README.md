# 🚖 Uber Ride Analytics 

## Project Objective
- Analyze ride demand and booking trends
- Understand customer preferences and behavior
- Identify factors affecting ride completion
- Evaluate revenue, ratings, and payment patterns
- Generate business insights for operational improvement

## Business Importance
- Understand customer demand and preferences
- Reduce ride cancellations
- Optimize driver allocation
- Improve customer satisfaction
- Support data-driven decision making

## Tools Used
- Python-(Pandas,NumPy,Matplotlib,Seaborn,Jupyter Notebook).

## Key Insights
- 62% ride completion rate
- UPI contributes ~45% of revenue
- Auto, Go Mini, and Go Sedan dominate ride demand
- High customer satisfaction ratings
- Driver cancellations remain the biggest operational challenge
